A Letter from the Field

For twenty years we have done one simple thing in Honduras: we show up, and we stay. We do not run programs. We sit in homes, open the Word, and become family.

We have watched fathers who once walked away learn to lead. Marriages mended. Churches built from nothing — in La Ceiba, Guaimaca, Talanga, Danli, and La Ermita. None of it is ours to claim. It is God turning the hearts of fathers back to their children.

We made these men a promise that we will not break: we will not leave them. We cannot keep it without you.

With you in the work, The Directors Fathers for the Fatherless · Honduras · Est. 2006

From the Field

God Is Working in a Mighty Way.

Many lives have already been changed. By God's grace, marriages are being saved through the principles of Scripture — and God is turning the hearts of fathers and their families to a deeper relationship with God and one another.

“Behold, I will send you Elijah the prophet — and he shall turn the heart of the fathers to the children, and the heart of the children to their fathers.”

— Malachi 4:5–6

We commit to starting with a strong foundation: training fathers to restore their marriages, love their wives and children, and lead their families in righteousness.

— Our Commitment to the Field

“Go ye therefore, and teach all nations — teaching them to observe all things whatsoever I have commanded you: and, lo, I am with you alway, even unto the end of the world.”

— Matthew 28:19–20

We train pastors to be examples to the flock, to disciple the fathers in their churches, and to preach the truths of the Word of God without compromise.

— Our Commitment to the Field

“And the things that thou hast heard of me — the same commit thou to faithful men, who shall be able to teach others also.”

— 2 Timothy 2:2

This work is only as strong as its foundation. Wherever God sends us — in Central America, the United States, or anywhere else — we build on the Word of God.

— Our Commitment to the Field
