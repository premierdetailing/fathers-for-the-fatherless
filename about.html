Who We Are

About Fathers for the Fatherless

A faith-based ministry committed to equipping fathers, planting churches, and restoring families in Honduras — since 2006.

The Story

Why We Exist.

God opened a great door in Honduras. The people there are hungry for the Word of God and the life-changing truths found in it. What started as a burden for fatherless families became a full ministry — boots on the ground, year after year, walking alongside men who had never been shown what it means to lead a family in righteousness.

We are not a relief organization. We are not a short-term trip company. We are a discipleship ministry. We come alongside men in Honduras, sit with them in their homes, open the Word of God together, and watch what happens when the Holy Spirit applies truth to a father's heart.

God is turning the hearts of fathers and families toward Him. Marriages are being saved. Children are growing up in homes led by men who love God and love their families. The work is real. The need is ongoing.

Our Approach

What Makes Us Different.

Most organizations work from a distance. We do not.

Long-Term Commitment

We do not parachute in and leave. Our directors have made multi-year relational commitments to specific men and specific communities. We return every year. Many of the men in Honduras are now dear friends.

Theological Foundation

We believe Scripture is sufficient. Our discipleship model is built on applying the truths of the Word of God to daily life — marriage, fatherhood, pastoral leadership, and worship. Gospel-centered from the ground up.

Boots on the Ground

The directors and officers of this ministry have personally traveled to Honduras and continue to do so. We do not fund from a distance and hope for the best. We go, we stay, we walk alongside.
