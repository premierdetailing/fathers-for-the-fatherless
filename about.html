About

A small team with a long commitment.

Every person involved has personally invested in this ministry, with their time, their money, and their presence on the field.

Portrait
H. Finnicum

Howard Finnicum

President

Has been training believers for parts of five decades. After labouring faithfully in a local church for most of that time, he now splits his time between Roanoke, Virginia and serving as the main overseer of the work in Honduras. He has three grown children and 16 grandchildren, and he serves God faithfully with his wife of over thirty years.

What makes the work what it is

Long-term

We go back every year. We measure the work in generations, not trips.

Theological

We open the Word. The teaching is the work, not a wrapper around it.

On the ground

A majority of our directors and officers have personally travelled to Honduras.

Where we work

Five cities, five men who stay.

Each location is led by a brother who lives among the people, knows their names, and does not leave.
