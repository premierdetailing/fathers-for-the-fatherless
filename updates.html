Spring 2026 Field Report

Trip Report

La Ceiba Mission House: Renovation Underway

The mission house in La Ceiba is in active renovation. Work is progressing steadily as we prepare it to serve as a permanent base of operations for church planting and discipleship work in the region. La Ceiba is Honduras's fourth most populous city and the eco-tourism capital of the country — a city of real strategic importance for the gospel.

Directors have personally traveled to assess progress, meet with the local men we are discipling, and plan the next phase of outreach. God is opening doors in La Ceiba that were not open before. This is the next major foothold for the work.

Field Reports

More God Stories.

These reports come directly from the work on the ground. Lives changed by the Word of God applied to real men in real homes.

2026 Family Restoration

Marriages Saved Through Scripture

By God's grace, several marriages that appeared beyond saving are being restored through the principles of God's Word taught directly to husbands and fathers. Men who came to the ministry broken — absent from their homes, disconnected from their children — are returning. They are leading. They are present.

2025 Leadership Training

A New Generation Rising in Danli

In Danli, younger men are engaging with the leadership training curriculum in a way that is deeply encouraging. These men are not just showing up — they are bringing their wives, their children. They are building the pattern of family worship and godly authority inside their own homes. This is how a generation changes.

2025 Church Planting

Churches Built on the Word — Not Programs

The work in Central Honduras continues to bear fruit. The local churches being established here are not built on programs, events, or personalities. They are local bodies of believers, trained in the Word, led by men accountable to God and to their communities. This is the kind of church that outlasts its founders.

2024 God Story

Fathers Turning Their Hearts Toward Home

We are rejoicing as men who once led passive, disconnected lives are returning to their Biblical roles. Husbands leading their households in righteousness. Fathers present at the table — physically, spiritually. Families worshipping and praying together. This is transformation starting at the foundation.

2024 Pastoral Development

Pastors Becoming Examples to Their Flocks

Twenty years of ministry has taught us that a church rises and falls on the character of its pastors. Through pastoral development work in Honduras, we are seeing men step into that role with sobriety and conviction — pastors who disciple the fathers in their churches and preach the truth without compromise.

2024 Annual Trip

Going Back, Every Year, Without Exception

Our directors and officers have personally traveled to Honduras year after year. The relationships we have built over years of return visits produce a depth of trust that short-term missions simply cannot replicate. Many of the men and families in Honduras are now dear friends. We go back because they expect us to. We intend to be there.
